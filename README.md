AI Job Market Analytics --- 2025 | Power BI

Project Overview

An interactive Power BI analytics project built using the AI Job
Market dataset (2025) from Kaggle. The project analyses 11,293 AI
job postings across 12 selected countries to understand hiring demand,
compensation, geography, workforce characteristics and hiring patterns.

The report was designed as a portfolio-focused business intelligence
project, emphasizing Power Query transformations, DAX measures,
interactive reporting and business storytelling.

Business Objectives

Analyse the scale and distribution of AI job demand across selected
markets.

Compare salary patterns across countries, regions, job categories
and experience bands.

Understand employment models, company characteristics and
remote-work patterns.

Examine hiring timelines and posting trends.

Enable country-level drill-down for deeper market analysis.

Project Limitations

The analysis covers 12 selected countries rather than the full
source dataset.

Findings describe the supplied Kaggle dataset and should not
automatically be interpreted as a complete representation of the
global AI labour market.

Job postings represent advertised demand, not necessarily successful
hires.

Hiring/application duration is based on the available posting and
application deadline fields.

Salary analysis is based on the dataset's salary values and derived
salary bands.
